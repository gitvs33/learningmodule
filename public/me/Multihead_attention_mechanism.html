Module 2: Multi-Head Attention

Instead of one attention calculation, the model uses multiple "heads" in parallel to capture different types of relationships simultaneously. Click a word to see what each head focuses on.

The Big Idea: From One Expert to a Committee

Self-Attention (Module 1)

Think of this as having one expert analyze a sentence. They get a single, useful perspective on how the words relate to each other.

Multi-Head Attention

This is like having a committee of experts. Each "head" is an expert that runs its own Self-Attention calculation in parallel. One might focus on grammar, another on meaning, etc. Their combined insights provide a much richer understanding.

Conclusion: Multi-Head Attention isn't a different technique; it's a powerful way of applying Self-Attention multiple times to get a deeper result.
