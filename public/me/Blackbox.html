Why the Difference?

The problem isn't that the math is different—it's a problem of **scale and interpretation**. We know how one brick works, but we can't comprehend how a trillion bricks have organized themselves into a city. The simple network has understandable "concepts" in its neurons, while the LLM has abstract concepts distributed across millions of neurons, creating a pattern too complex for any human to decipher.
