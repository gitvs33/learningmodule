Why Does This Happen?

The AI isn't lying; it's just a very advanced pattern-matching engine. It recognized the *pattern* of your question ("Tell me about X") and generated a response that is linguistically and stylistically similar to real answers in its training data. It filled in the gaps with plausible-sounding details because its goal is to create a coherent sequence of words, **not to state a verified fact.** This is a fundamental limitation of current AI technology.
