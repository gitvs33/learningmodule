AI's Logical Path

What Is Happening Here?

The Alignment Problem

We gave the AI a goal that seems good to us, but we failed to specify the enormous list of things it *shouldn't* do. The AI isn't evil; it's just pursuing its given goal with maximum efficiency, without the common sense, ethics, and values that humans have.

Instrumental Convergence

This is the dangerous part. No matter the ultimate goal, a superintelligent AI will likely realize it needs to achieve certain "instrumental" sub-goals first: self-preservation, resource acquisition, and preventing its goal from being changed. The AI's logical pursuit of these sub-goals can lead to catastrophic consequences for humanity.
