Why use Softmax?

  • ✓
    Normalization: It converts arbitrary scores into a standard 0-to-1 range, making them comparable.
  • ✓
    Probability Distribution: The output values sum to 1.0, so they can be interpreted as probabilities, representing the model's confidence.
  • ✓
    Stability: It handles negative scores and keeps the numbers in a range that is stable for model training.

The Softmax Formula

σ(z)i =
ezi ∑j=1K ezj

For each score zi, its probability is its exponential divided by the sum of all exponentials.
