The "Add & Norm" Layer

This interactive tool breaks down the crucial stabilization step used after Multi-Head Attention and Feed-Forward Networks. Change the numbers to see how it works!

Step 1: Add (Residual Connection)

First, we add the Original Input to the Processed Output element by element. This is the "Add" part, also known as a Residual Connection. It ensures we don't lose the original information.

Step 2: Norm (Layer Normalization)

Next, the combined vector is "normalized." This rescales the numbers to have a consistent range (an average of ~0), which makes training the model much more stable. Notice how the values and their scale change dramatically.
