How It Works: Expand and Contract

The FFN is a very simple, two-step neural network:

  • 1.
    Expansion Layer: It first projects the vector into a much larger dimension (e.g., from 512 to 2048). This gives the model a bigger "workspace" to find and separate complex patterns.
  • बीच में
    ReLU Activation: After expanding, any negative numbers are set to zero. This simple non-linear step is crucial for the network to learn complex, non-linear relationships. (Notice in the visualizer how negative inputs become zero in the hidden layer).
  • 2.
    Contraction Layer: It then projects the large vector back down to the original dimension (e.g., from 2048 back to 512). This consolidates the patterns learned in the larger space.

Why Is It Used?

Think of it like a private study session after a group discussion.

  • ✓
    Group Discussion (Attention): The Multi-Head Attention layer is where all the words talk to each other and share context.
  • ✓
    Private Study (FFN): After the discussion, each word takes its newly enriched vector to the FFN for individual processing. The FFN acts like a "thought" process, finding deeper meaning and abstract connections within that single vector before the next group discussion (the next Encoder Layer).
