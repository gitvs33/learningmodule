The New Components

Feed-Forward Network (FFN)

After the attention mechanism has mixed information between words, the FFN processes each word's vector independently. Its job is to perform further calculations and transformations on that vector, allowing the model to learn more complex patterns.

Add & Norm (Residuals & Normalization)

This is a crucial trick for training deep networks.
Add: We add the input of a block to its output. This "residual connection" helps prevent the model from losing the original information as it passes through layers.
Norm: Layer Normalization rescales the numbers to a standard range, which keeps the training process stable.
