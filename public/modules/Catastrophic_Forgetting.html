What Happened?

When you trained the network on the second task, it adjusted all its internal weights to become an expert at that new task. In doing so, it **overwrote the specific weight configuration** that made it an expert on the first task. The old knowledge was lost.

This is unlike humans. Learning a new skill, like playing the violin, doesn't make you forget a previously learned one, like playing the guitar. Overcoming this "catastrophic forgetting" is a critical area of research to build truly adaptive and lifelong learning AI systems.
