What's Happening Here?

You just witnessed an **emergent ability**. The model wasn't explicitly taught arithmetic. However, by learning the statistical patterns of language on a massive scale, it implicitly learned the relationships that govern math.

This ability doesn't grow smoothly. It appears to "switch on" suddenly, a phenomenon similar to a **phase transition** in physics (like water suddenly freezing into ice). This suggests that quantitative increases in scale can lead to qualitative leaps in capability, making the future of AI incredibly powerful but also difficult to predict.
